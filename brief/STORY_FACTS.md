# Story facts (the truth every screen must agree with)

Product: onchain.cc Funded. The trader competes with their own money in a 30-day qualification window. If they qualify and score 70 or more, they request a seat and trade our USDC, keeping 80% of profit.

## Rules
- **Get on the leaderboard:** deposit $100 and trade $1,000 of perps volume.
- **Qualify:** 20 closed trades, 15 trading days, in profit so far, and max drawdown of 25% or less. "Qualified" means all four are met.
- **Score:** 0 to 100.
- **Seats:** 77 and above gets a $25K seat (5,000 USDC, $25,000 position cap). 70 to 76 gets a $5K seat (1,000 USDC, $5,000 cap). Below 70, no seat. 61 seats in total, $145,000 of USDC.
- **Request window:** 24 hours after scores freeze. If seats run out, you join the waitlist. A freed seat offer gives 48h to accept.
- **Funded seat:**
  - Daily pause at $1,250 lost in a day ($250 on a $5K seat).
  - The seat closes at $2,500 lost ($500 on a $5K seat), or after 7 days in a row below its start.
- **Payouts:**
  - Any time the seat is flat: all profit above the start, 80% to the trader and 20% to us, paid within 72h.
  - The seat then resets to its start.
  - Payouts stay on the board and profile for good.
- One seat at a time. There is no KYC. Never mention Bitso or Hyperliquid, and never link off the site.

## Words
- Use "Qualification" in chips and labels, and "qualification window" in sentences. Never number them.
- Use "Request window" for the 24 hours.
- Use "Payout", never "claim".
- Use "Seat" for the funded side, and "My account" or "your own account" for the trader's money.
- Use "Score to beat 70". Never say "the bar".
- Never: epoch, season, liquidation, lock point, percentile, pilot.
