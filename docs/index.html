funded.onchain.cc user flows

9 independent flows, 52 navigable screens with demo data. Every trader competes with their own account in a dated cycle; when it closes, those who meet the requirements request a seat within 24 hours and seats are assigned by score. Bitso provides the USDC of every funded account.

F01

Onboarding

5 screens

Open
What it does
A visitor arrives from X, a creator or a referral link, understands the offer, sees the live leaderboard and the rules, and signs in with the same account they use on onchain.cc.
Why
It is the first contact and has to convince a professional trader in seconds: Bitso's real money, 80% of the profit, no challenge fees and clear rules.
  1. 1LandingWhat is this and why should I care?
  2. 2Public leaderboardWho is winning seats right now?
  3. 3Public payoutsDoes this actually pay? Who got paid, how much and when?
  4. 4RulesWhat exactly do I have to do, and what can I lose?
  5. 5Sign inHow do I get in? Same account as onchain.cc.
F02

Not qualified yet

6 screens

Open
What it does
The trader has signed in but does not meet the minimums to appear on the leaderboard yet: they see what is missing, deposit into their own account and choose an alias.
Why
Many arrive with no history. Without a short, concrete path to the leaderboard they leave before trading.
  1. 1Home, nothing met yetWhat do I need to do to get on the board?
  2. 2DepositHow do I fund my own trading account?
  3. 3Home, in progressHow close am I?
  4. 4Home, on the leaderboardI made it. Where do I stand?
  5. 5Choose an aliasHow will others see me on the board?
  6. 6Home, new to onchainI have never traded on onchain.cc. When can I realistically request a seat?
F03

Competing

8 screens

Open
What it does
The trader trades their own money during the cycle and follows their standing: board rank and seat, the seat map, score, stats and the terminal for their own account.
Why
This is where they spend most of their time and where the volume that pays for the business is generated. They must always know whether they are inside or outside the seats and what they are missing.
  1. 1Home, competingAm I inside the seats, and what moves my score?
  2. 2LeaderboardWhere am I against everyone else?
  3. 3Leaderboard, around the cutWho is fighting for the last seats, and how far am I from them?
  4. 4Trader profileHow is the trader above me doing it?
  5. 5Score breakdownHow is my score built and what would raise it?
  6. 6Trade, own accountTrade with my own money while the cycle runs.
  7. 7StatsHow good is my trading, really?
  8. 8PayoutsWho is getting paid from their funded accounts, and how much?
F04

Seat claim

9 screens

Open
What it does
When the cycle closes there are 24 hours to request a seat; the live request board shows who has requested, and when it ends, who gets an account and who does not.
Why
It is the decisive moment of every cycle. It has to be transparent so nobody doubts how seats are assigned.

  4. 4Live request boardWho has requested so far, and where does the cut sit right now?
  5. 5Home, cannot requestWhy can't I request a seat this time?
  6. 6Home, already fundedI already hold an account. What does the cycle mean for me?
  7. 7Seat granted, account liveI got a seat. What exactly do I get, and can I trade now?
  8. 8No seatWhy didn't I get one, and when is the next chance?
  9. 9Request window missedI forgot to request. What now?
F05

Operating the funded account

6 screens

Open
What it does
The trader trades Bitso's USDC from the same terminal, with the trailing liquidation level always in view, and can switch to their own account without mixing figures.
Why
A professional needs to see on every order how much room is left before losing the account. That is what sets this product apart from any other terminal.
  1. 1Home, funded accountHow is my account doing against its rules?
  2. 2Trade, funded accountTrade within the rules, with the risk of each order shown before I send it.
  3. 3Order on a market not allowedWhy can't I send this order?
  4. 4Switch accountMove between my own account and the funded one.
  5. 5Order placedMy order went in. What did it change in my limits?
  6. 6Liquidation locked at the startI'm 10% up. Where is my liquidation level now, and why won't it rise further?
F06

Risk events

2 screens

Open
What it does
The two hard moments: the account liquidated when equity reaches its level, and a platform outage that closes positions while the account stays active.
Why
These are the moments where trust is won or lost. They must explain what happened, what the trader keeps and what they can do next, without ambiguity.
  1. 1Account liquidatedMy equity reached the liquidation level. What happened and what do I keep?
  2. 2Platform incidentOrders can't reach Hyperliquid. What happened to my positions and my account?
F07

Claims

5 screens

Open
What it does
The trader claims whenever they want, with every position closed: 80% of the profit goes to their own account and the funded account returns to its starting size.
Why
Getting paid is the promise of the product. If it is not clear how much is claimed and what happens to the account afterwards, nobody trusts the rest.
  1. 1Profit to claimHow much can I claim now, and what does claiming do to my account?
  2. 2Claim profitConfirm the claim: all profit, 80% to my own account.
  3. 3Profit claimedDid it reach my own account, and where does my funded account stand now?
  4. 4Close positions to claimI have open positions. What do I need to do to claim?
  5. 5Nothing to claimMy account is not above its start. What can I do?
F08

After the account

3 screens

Open
What it does
After losing the account, the trader goes back to competing with their own money and can request a seat again at the next cycle close, with no penalty.
Why
Keeping the trader who loses is key to growth: every return to the competition brings more volume and more referrals.
  1. 1Home after losing the accountMy account is gone. How do I get another one?
  2. 2Competing againI'm back on the board. Does my last account count against me?
  3. 3Request a seat againCycle 2 closed. Can I request a seat now?
F09

Deposit and withdraw

8 screens

Open
What it does
The own account: the balance and where it came from, USDC deposits from any EVM chain or Solana to a personal address, withdrawals to any EVM address, and what happens when a token that is not USDC arrives.
Why
Without their own money there is no competition. Moving funds in and out has to be as simple as on an exchange, and clearly separate from the funded account.
  1. 1Own account balanceHow much do I have, and where did it come from?
  2. 2DepositWhere do I send funds, from which chain, and how long does it take?
  3. 3Deposit arrivingI sent it. Where is my money now?
  4. 4Deposit arrivedIs it in my trading balance?
  5. 5WithdrawHow do I send money out, to which address and network?
  6. 6Confirm withdrawalIs everything right before it leaves?
  7. 7Withdrawal sentDid it leave, and when will it arrive?
  8. 8Deposit not creditedI sent a token that is not USDC. What happened to it?
