FundedFlow mockups for funded.onchain.cc. Demo data only.

funded.onchain.cc user flows

9 independent flows, 57 navigable screens with demo data. Every trader competes with their own account during a 30-day qualification window. When it ends, qualified traders request a seat within 24 hours and seats go by score. onchain.cc Funded provides the USDC of every seat.

F01

Onboarding

6 screens

Open
What it does
A visitor arrives from X, a creator or a referral link, sees the offer, the live leaderboard, the funded traders and payouts, reads how it works and signs up with Google, Apple, email or a wallet, with the referral code already filled in.
Why
It is the first contact and has to convince a trader in seconds: free to enter, real USDC, 80% of the profit and every payout public.

  2. 2Leaderboard, qualificationWho would get a seat right now, and what score do I need?
  3. 3Leaderboard, funded tradersWho is trading a seat right now, and how is it going?
  4. 4Leaderboard, payoutsDoes this actually pay? Who got paid, and how much?
  5. 5How it worksWhat do I have to do, and how does a seat work?
  6. 6Sign upHow do I get in? Same account as onchain.cc, invite code already in.

Getting on the board

Open
What it does
The trader has signed up but is not on the leaderboard yet. Two steps get them there: add $100 and trade $1,000 of perps volume. Then they choose an alias.

Many arrive with no history. A short, concrete path to the board keeps them from leaving before the first trade.

F05

Operating the funded account

6 screens

Open
What it does
The trader trades our USDC from the same terminal, with the daily pause and the loss room always in view, and switches to their own account without mixing figures.
Why
A trader needs to see on every order how much of today's pause and the seat's loss room it uses. That is what sets this apart from any other terminal.

  2. 2Trade on the seatHow much of today's pause would this order use?
  3. 3Market not allowedWhy can't I send this order on the seat?
  4. 4Switch accountMove between my own account and the seat.
  5. 5Near the daily pause$150 left before today's pause. What happens if I reach it?
  6. 6Paused for the dayNew trades stopped. What can I still do, and when does it lift?

These are the moments where trust is won or lost. They must say what happened, what the trader keeps and what comes next, calmly and clearly.

F07

Payouts

5 screens

Open
What it does
The trader requests a payout any time the seat is flat: 80% of the profit goes to their own account and the seat resets to its start.
Why
Getting paid is the promise of the product. If it is not clear how much is paid and what happens to the seat afterwards, nobody trusts the rest.
  1. 1Payout readyHow much can I take now, and what happens to my seat?
  2. 2Request a payoutKeep trading or close the seat, then confirm.
  3. 3Payout sentDid it reach my own account, and where does my seat stand now?
  4. 4Close positions firstI have an open position. What do I need to do first?
  5. 5No payout yetMy seat is not above its start. What can I do?
F08

After the account

3 screens

Open
What it does
After a seat closes, the trader keeps their payouts, goes back to competing with their own money and can request a seat again when the next qualification window ends, with no penalty.
Why
Keeping the trader whose seat closed is key to growth: every return brings more volume and more referrals.
  1. 1Account after the seat closedMy seat closed. How do I get another one?
  2. 2Competing againI'm back on the board. Does my last seat count against me?
  3. 3Request a seat againThe qualification window ended. Can I request a seat now?
F09

Deposit and withdraw

8 screens

Open
What it does
The own account: the balance and where it came from, adding funds by card or crypto, withdrawals to any EVM address, and what happens when a token that is not USDC arrives.
Why
Without their own money there is no competition. Moving funds in and out has to be as simple as on an exchange, and clearly separate from the seat.
