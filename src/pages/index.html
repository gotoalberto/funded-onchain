funded.onchain.cc user flows

9 independent flows, 57 navigable screens with demo data. Every trader competes with their own account in their own 30-day window, starting the day they clear the entry gate. The frozen score sets one of six tiers, and a freed seat is offered to the highest score in contention in that tier. onchain.cc Funded provides the USDC of every seat.

Onboarding

A visitor arrives from X, a creator or a referral link, sees the offer, the live leaderboard, the funded traders and payouts, reads how it works and signs up with Google, Apple, email or a wallet, with the referral code already filled in.

It is the first contact and has to convince a trader in seconds: free to enter, real USDC, 80% of the profit and every payout public.

F02

Getting on the board

9 screens

Open
What it does
The trader has signed up but is not on the leaderboard yet. The entry gate gets them there: $100 deposited, $1,000 of volume and 10 closed trades. Clearing it starts their own 30-day window. Then they choose an alias.
Why
Many arrive with no history. A short, concrete path to the board keeps them from leaving before the first trade.

F04

Seat offers

9 screens

Open
What it does
On day 30 the score freezes and sets the tier. A seat is offered the moment one frees up to the highest score in contention in that tier, with 48 hours to accept and funding on the daily run at 14:00 UTC. Every outcome says what happens next.
Why
It is the decisive moment. It has to be transparent so nobody doubts how seats are offered.

F07

Payouts

5 screens

Open
What it does
The trader takes a payout any time the seat is flat: 80% of the profit goes to their own account and the seat resets to its start.
Why
Getting paid is the promise of the product. If it is not clear how much is paid and what happens to the seat afterwards, nobody trusts the rest.

F08

After the account

3 screens

Open
What it does
After a seat closes, the trader keeps their payouts, goes back to competing with their own money in their next window and is back in contention when a window qualifies again, with no penalty.
Why
Keeping the trader whose seat closed is key to growth: every return brings more volume and more referrals.
