How it works

No shared round to wait for. Your own 30 days, and your score decides your seat.

  1. Your window is your ownIt starts when you clear the entry gate, not when a group does.
  2. Trade for 30 daysYour score moves as you trade. On day 30 it freezes.
  3. Your score sets your tierQualify at 50. Every 6 points steps you up a seat, to the top tier at 80.
  4. Offered the moment a seat frees upThe top score in the tier gets it. Accepted seats are funded on a daily run.
  5. Qualification lasts 30 daysKeep trading and your next window renews it.
  6. Get paidKeep 80% of the seat's profit, any time you are flat.

1. Entry

Your 30-day window starts once you clear the entry gate.

  • $100deposited
  • $1,000of volume traded
  • 10closed trades

Everyone who cleared the gate before launch started their window on launch day, Oct 1. From then on, every trader starts on the day they clear the gate.

2. Your window

Length
30 days from your start date.
Fixed
You cannot restart a window early. Your score freezes on day 30.
Back to back
If you do not qualify, your next window starts the following day. No gap, no waiting for a new round.

The score runs from 0 to 100. Your frozen score on day 30 places you in a tier. The tier sets the USDC you trade.

The score is built from three parts of your own account.

Volume, trade count, win rate and balance are not part of it. Withdrawals never affect your score. There are no other requirements: the score alone decides.

4. Qualification lasts 30 days

  • A qualifying score keeps you in contention for a seat in your tier for 30 days.
  • Keep trading and your next window renews your qualification. A trader who keeps scoring stays in contention without a break.
  • Stop trading and your qualification lapses after 30 days.

5. How seats are offered

When
The moment a seat frees up. No offer days, no waiting for a batch.
Who gets the offer
The qualified, active trader with the highest score in that tier. Not first come, first served: the score decides.
48 hours to accept
If you do not accept, the seat goes to the next highest score in the tier.
A lower seat
If a seat in a lower tier is offered to you first, you can take it now. You keep your qualification for your own tier while it is valid.
One seat at a time
While you hold a seat you get no other offers.
Funding
Accepted seats are checked and funded once a day, on the daily run at 14:00 UTC. You wait at most a day between accepting and trading.

A daily pause stops new trades until 00:00 UTC. Closing is always allowed. The most a seat can lose is its USDC. At 10x, the daily pause comes at a 2.5% price move against you and the seat closes at a 5% move.

Margin
Cross margin only.
Allowed markets
Deep crypto and RWA markets: BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE, gold, oil, US500, NVDA, TSLA.
What blocks an order
Off-list markets, isolated margin, going above 10x, a daily pause.
Where you trade
The seat trades only from funded.onchain.cc and onchain.cc.
How long
No end date. The seat stays yours until it closes or you close it.

7. Payouts

  • 80%of profit to you, 20% to onchain.cc
  • Any timethe seat has no open positions
  • Resetthe seat goes back to its start

An example on a $2,500 seat. The balance reaches $2,962, a profit of $462. You get $369.60 in your own account and we get $92.40. The seat then resets to 2,500 USDC.

Every payout stays on the public Payouts board and your profile for good, even after a seat closes.

Common questions

Can I lose my own money?
Only what you lose trading, like any account. Entry is free.
Every seat in my tier is taken?
You stay in contention while your qualification is valid. The next freed seat goes to the top score.
Do I have to wait for a round?
No. Your window starts the day you clear the gate, and the next one starts the day after.
Is any of this simulated?
No. Real markets, real USDC, and every payout verified onchain.

Other rules

Platform incidents
If trading goes down on our side, seat positions close automatically and the seat stays safe. You can take a payout once flat.
While funded
Your own account keeps trading and stays on the board. You get no offers while you hold a seat.
Referrals
Earn 10% of your friends' trading fees, plus 0.01% of the volume traded on any seat they're funded with.
Regions
Wherever onchain.cc is available. No documents needed.
