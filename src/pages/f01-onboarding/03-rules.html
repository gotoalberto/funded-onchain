Get started

How it works

Trade perps with your own money. Keep every dollar of profit.

  1. Join the leaderboardDeposit $100 and trade $1,000 of perps volume. Then your score counts.
  2. Climb for 30 daysYour score rewards steady, risk-aware trading. Not volume.
  3. QualifyMeet four requirements before scores freeze.
  4. Request a seatYou have 24 hours once scores freeze. Seats go by score.
  5. Get fundedA score of 77 or more gets a $25K seat. 70 to 76 gets a $5K seat.
  6. Get paidKeep 80% of the profit.

Common questions

What is my score?
A number from 0 to 100. See how it works.
Can I lose my own money?
Only what you lose trading, like any account. Entry is free.
What if seats run out?
You join the waitlist and get offered the next free seat.
Is any of this simulated?
No. Real markets, real USDC, and every payout verified onchain.

Requirements

Everything is measured on your own account.

Max drawdown 25% or less

Max drawdown is your biggest drop from a high. Go past 25% and you keep your rank and your profit, but sit out the seats until the next qualification window.

A trading day is a UTC day with at least one trade opened or closed. Your onchain.cc trades since Oct 20 already count.

How the score works

A whole number from 0 to 100, built from three parts of your own account.

PartWhat it rewardsWeight
Return for the riskProfit for the risk you took75%
Worst tradeKeeping your biggest loss small15%
SteadinessGains spread across many days10%

Volume, trade count, win rate and balance are not part of it. Withdrawals never affect your score. It refreshes every minute and freezes when the qualification window ends.

Seats

61 seats on Nov 19, $145,000 of USDC.

  1. Qualified and at 70 or above? Request a seat. One tick accepts the seat terms. Cancel your request any time before the request window closes.
  2. If seats run out before your rank, you join the waitlist. When a seat frees up, the next trader on the list has 48 hours to accept. Passing keeps your place.
  3. Below 70, or short of a requirement? Try again in the next qualification window. Your own profit is yours either way.

Choose to take the payout and keep trading, or take the payout and close the seat. Skipping a payout is fine. The profit stays in the seat.

Trading the seat

Margin
Cross margin only.
Allowed markets
Deep crypto and RWA markets: BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE, gold, oil, US500, NVDA, TSLA.
What blocks an order
Off-list markets, isolated margin, going over the cap, a daily pause.
Where you trade
The seat trades only from funded.onchain.cc and onchain.cc.
How long
No end date. The seat stays yours until it closes or you close it.

Other rules

Platform incidents
If trading goes down on our side, seat positions close automatically and the seat stays safe. You can take a payout once flat.
One seat at a time
While funded, your own account stays on the board but cannot request another seat. If your seat closes, you can request again when the next qualification window ends.
Referrals
Earn 10% of your friends' trading fees, plus 0.01% of the volume traded on any seat they're funded with.
Regions
Wherever onchain.cc is available. No documents needed.
