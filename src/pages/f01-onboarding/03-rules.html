Rules

No fee, no application. Bitso funds 60 accounts in Cycle 1.

Two accounts

Separate accounts. Their numbers are never added together.

OwnYour own account

Your money, in your onchain.cc Hyperliquid account.

  • {{icon:trophy:ico-sm}}Competes in the cycle
  • {{icon:hand-coins:ico-sm}}Receives your 80% of claims
  • {{icon:shield-check:ico-sm}}At risk: only your trading losses. We liquidate nothing.
  • {{icon:circle-slash:ico-sm}}Breaking the drawdown requirement rules out a request that cycle

FundedThe funded account

Bitso's USDC, in a separate account you trade.

  • {{icon:arrow-up-from-line:ico-sm}}Money leaves only through a claim
  • {{icon:infinity:ico-sm}}No end date
  • {{icon:trending-down:ico-sm}}At risk: the account, at the trailing liquidation level. Claimed profit stays yours.

Drawdown requirement

Equity, unrealized included, may never fall 10% of your starting capital below your highest equity. Once 10% up, your line locks at the starting capital. Deposits and withdrawals move the starting capital, not your result.

{{icon:circle-slash:ico-sm}}Touching the line rules out a seat request this cycle. It closes nothing.

Trailing liquidation

The funded account's only loss rule. On a $25K account the level starts at $22,500, stays $2,500 (10% of the $25,000 size) below your highest equity and stops rising at $25,000, once equity reaches $27,500.

  • {{icon:activity:ico-sm}}Equity at Hyperliquid's mark price, unrealized included. No daily limit
  • {{icon:x-circle:ico-sm}}Reaching it closes the account for good, at real closing prices. The rest returns to Bitso
  • {{icon:rotate-ccw:ico-sm}}A claim resets the level
